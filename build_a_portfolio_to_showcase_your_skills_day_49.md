# Coding Tip #49: Build a Portfolio to Showcase Your Skills

A portfolio is a great way to demonstrate your skills to potential employers or clients. It gives you a platform to showcase your projects, code, and accomplishments.

**Tip**: Create a simple yet polished portfolio site where you can highlight your best work.

Example of portfolio items:
- Personal projects or side projects that showcase your skills.
- Contributions to open-source projects.
- A blog where you share coding tutorials, experiences, or challenges.

A well-crafted portfolio will help you stand out and give you the confidence to apply for jobs or collaborate on projects.
