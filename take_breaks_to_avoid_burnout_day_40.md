# Coding Tip #40: Take Breaks to Avoid Burnout

Coding for long hours without breaks can lead to burnout, decreased productivity, and frustration. Taking regular breaks helps you stay focused and maintain a fresh perspective.

**Tip**: Follow the `Pomodoro Technique` or set regular intervals to step away from your screen. It’s not only good for mental health but also boosts long-term productivity.

Example of the Pomodoro Technique:
```js
const pomodoro = (duration = 25, breakTime = 5) => {
  console.log('Starting work session...');
  setTimeout(() => {
    console.log("Take a break for 25 minutes!");
  }, duration * 60 * 1000);
};

pomodoro();
```

Don’t be afraid to step away for a bit—your code will thank you for it.
