# Coding Tip #46: Manage Your Time Effectively

Time management is a vital skill for programmers. Whether you’re working on a side project, freelance work, or a full-time job, managing your time efficiently will help you stay productive and avoid burnout.

**Tip**: Use time management techniques like the `Pomodoro Technique` or time-blocking to structure your workday.

Example of time blocking:
```js
// Block your day for focused work
const workSchedule = {
  '9am-11am': 'Focus on coding',
  '11am-12pm': 'Check emails',
  '1pm-3pm': 'Work on project presentation',
  '3pm-5pm': 'Debug and fix issues'
};

console.log(workSchedule);
```

By managing your time effectively, you’ll make steady progress without feeling overwhelmed.
